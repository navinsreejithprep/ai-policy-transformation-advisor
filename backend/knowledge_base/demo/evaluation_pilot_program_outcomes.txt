Pilot Evaluation Report (Synthetic): District-Level Digital Access Points

Scope: A fictional 6-month pilot of three digital access points in a subset of
districts, evaluated against three illustrative KPIs.

KPI 1 — Task completion rate without escalation to in-person channel: rose over
the pilot period among first-time users in this synthetic evaluation.

KPI 2 — Average assisted sessions before independent completion: fell from an
initial baseline as facilitators gained experience, though it did not reach the
single-session target set at pilot start.

[PAGE 2]
KPI 3 — Citizen satisfaction (self-reported, synthetic survey): satisfaction was
higher among users who had access to a facilitator on their first visit than
among users who attempted self-service without support first.

Evaluation caveat: sample sizes in this fictional pilot were small (under 500
users per site) and results should be read as directional, not statistically
robust — this caveat is itself part of the synthetic demo content, to model
how a benchmark agent should discuss evidence limitations.

Recommendation (from the fictional pilot evaluators): scale the facilitator
model before scaling pure self-service capacity, and set the single-session
independent-completion target as a longer-term goal rather than a year-one KPI.
