Post-Implementation Case Study (Synthetic): Regional Service Modernisation Pilot

Summary: A fictional prior pilot with a similar aim (moving citizen services
online) experienced a six-month delivery delay and a data-handling incident
during rollout. This case study is synthetic and constructed to give the Risk
Analyst agent something concrete to reference in the demo.

Vendor and delivery risk

The pilot's technology vendor missed two milestone deadlines due to
underestimated integration effort with the legacy case-management system. No
contractual penalty clause existed for schedule slippage, which removed any
commercial incentive to recover the timeline.

[PAGE 2]
Data risk incident

A misconfigured access control on an intermediate database exposed a subset of
applicant records to internal staff outside the intended case-handling team for
approximately three weeks before detection, in this fictional scenario. No
external breach occurred, but it triggered an internal review and delayed the
next rollout phase by eight weeks.

Adoption risk

Frontline staff in the fictional pilot reported that the initial training
covered system navigation but not exception-handling ("what do I do when the
system rejects a valid case"), which led to inconsistent workarounds across
different local offices.

[PAGE 3]
Mitigations applied in the fictional case (for reference)

- Added milestone-linked payment terms to the vendor contract following the
  delay.
- Introduced a mandatory access-control review gate before each environment
  promotion.
- Extended frontline training to include exception-handling scenarios and
  designated a local super-user per office.
