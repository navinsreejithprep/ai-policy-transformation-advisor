Citizen advocacy groups: Supported the programme's goals but pushed back on a

similar past fictional programmes had previously led to underestimating

Pilot case study).

Frontline service-centre staff: Asked for exception-handling training to be
included from the start, not added after go-live, referencing recurring
problems from a comparable fictional past rollout.

Overall sentiment (analyst characterization, not a verbatim quote): stakeholders
were broadly supportive of the programme's objective but skeptical of a
digital-only delivery model without adequate staffing, training and a
guaranteed fallback channel.
